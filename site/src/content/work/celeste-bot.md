Celeste-bot is a research project exploring how machine learning techniques can be applied to play [Celeste](https://www.celestegame.com/), a challenging precision platformer. The project investigates training AI agents to navigate the game's demanding levels.

Learn more at [projectzoran.com](https://projectzoran.com/).
